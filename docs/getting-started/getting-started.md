# Getting Started with Yarpad

Welcome to Yarpad!

Yarpad helps private teachers organize their lessons, students, schedules, and payment information in one place.

## Who is Yarpad for?

**Teachers** can use Yarpad to manage their teaching activities, including:

- Creating a teacher profile
- Configuring working hours and availability
- Managing students and lesson schedules
- Defining booking and cancellation policies
- Organizing lesson pricing and payment information

**Students** can use Yarpad to:

- Connect with their teacher
- View available lesson times
- Book lessons
- Manage their scheduled lessons
- View their teacher's payment information

## Create your account

1. Visit [Yarpad](https://yarpad.app).
2. Register an account.
3. Activate your account using the confirmation email.
4. Sign in to Yarpad.

For the complete teacher registration and activation flow, see [Create and Activate Your Teacher Account](../teachers/getting-started/create-account.md).

!!! important

    Students register through their teacher's registration link, not the teacher registration page.

Teachers can then complete their initial setup, including their profile, working hours, and lesson policies.
