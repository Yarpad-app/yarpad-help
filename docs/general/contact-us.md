# Contact Us

Contact us is available to signed-in Yarpad users. Both teachers and students
can use it. You must sign in before you can open the Contact Us form.

## Open Contact Us

1. Sign in to Yarpad.
2. Open the navigation menu.
3. Select **Contact us**.

## Complete the Contact Form

The form has two required fields:

- **Subject** — summarize your message. It is required and must contain 1–100
  characters, with a maximum of 100 characters.
- **Description** — describe the issue or feedback. It is required and must
  contain 1–1000 characters, with a maximum of 1000 characters.

Provide enough information in the Subject and Description to explain your
request clearly.

## Send a Message

1. Enter a **Subject**.
2. Enter a **Description**.
3. Select the send icon. Its tooltip is **Contact us**.
4. Wait for the confirmation message.

The send action is not labeled **Submit**. Wait for the confirmation before
leaving the page or sending another message.

## After Submission

Yarpad processes the submitted message. Your authenticated user information is
included automatically, so you do not need to enter your email address.

After successful processing, the app displays this confirmation:

> Your message has been sent successfully.  
> Thank you for sharing your feedback with us.  
> We appreciate your input.

After you acknowledge the confirmation, the app navigates away from the
Contact Us form.

The confirmation does not promise an email, a support ticket number, a reply,
a response time, or message tracking.

## Validation and Troubleshooting

### “Subject is required”

Enter a Subject before selecting the send icon.

### “Description is required”

Enter a Description before selecting the send icon.

### Subject or Description is too long

The Subject must contain 1–100 characters. The Description must contain
1–1000 characters. Shorten the field that exceeds its limit, then try again.

### The message has not been confirmed

Wait for the in-app confirmation after selecting the send icon. If the
submission fails, check the error message, your network connection, and that
you are still authenticated.

Do not send the message repeatedly when you do not know whether the previous
submission was successful. First check the result shown by the app.
