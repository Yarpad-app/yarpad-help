# How to Add a Student to Yarpad

You can add a student in either of these ways:

- **Share your public profile link** so the student enters their own
  information and chooses a password.
- **Enroll the student yourself** by entering the student's name and email.
  Yarpad creates the account and generates a temporary password.

Both methods require the student to activate their account by completing
**Email Verification**. The student does not appear in **My students** until
activation is complete.

## Choose the right method

| Method | What happens | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share your public profile link | The student enters their own information, chooses a password, and completes email verification. | The student can register personally and you want them to choose their password. |
| Enroll the student yourself | You enter the student's name and email. Yarpad generates a temporary password and queues a welcome email. The student signs in and completes email verification. | You already have the student's details and want to start the account setup for them. |

## Option A: Share your public profile

Your profile must be published before its public URL will work. For more
information about your profile, see [Create and Manage Your Teacher
Profile](teacher-profile.md).

1. Sign in to Yarpad as a teacher.
2. Open **Settings** and select **Profile**.
3. Complete your teacher profile.
4. Select **Save and Publish**.
5. Locate the generated public profile link.
6. Select the copy icon.
7. Paste the link into a message to the student.
8. Ask the student to open the link and select **Register for class**.
9. The student completes **Student account registration** and chooses a
   password.
10. The student signs in and completes **Email Verification** when prompted.

The student can review the detailed process in [Register as a
Student](../../student/register-as-student.md). Do not send the student to a
generic teacher-registration page.

If your profile is a draft or hidden, it is not publicly accessible. Selecting
**Save and Publish** makes it public. If an already published profile is
updated with **Save**, the changes become public without publishing again.

## Option B: Enroll a student yourself

1. Sign in to Yarpad as a teacher.
2. Open **My students**.
3. Select **Enrollment**.
4. Enter the student's **Full name** and **Email**.
5. Select **Register**.
6. Yarpad creates the student account with a temporary password.
7. Yarpad queues a welcome email containing the login credentials. Email
   delivery may take time.
8. The student signs in and completes **Email Verification** when prompted.
9. After activation, the student can appear in **My students**.

You do not choose or see the student's temporary password. This enrollment
form creates a new student account; it does not attach an already existing
Yarpad account. There is no pending-student list for this workflow. For the
detailed enrollment instructions, see [Enroll a Student](enroll-student.md).

## What happens after registration?

Both methods create the teacher-student association before email activation.
New student accounts are initially inactive. When an inactive student tries
to sign in, Yarpad starts the activation flow and sends an activation email.
The email contains a nine-digit code.

The student enters the code in **Email Verification**. After successful
activation, the student can sign in normally. **My students** excludes
inactive students, so successful registration or enrollment alone does not
make the student visible there. You do not need to approve an activated
account separately.

Activation email delivery is not necessarily immediate. The student should
check their inbox and spam folder if the message has not arrived.

## Why can't I see my student?

- Ask whether the student completed **Email Verification**.
- Ask the student to check their inbox and spam folder.
- Explain that successful enrollment does not make an inactive student
  visible in **My students**.
- An existing Yarpad account cannot simply be attached through the enrollment
  form.
- For the detailed steps, see [Enroll a Student](enroll-student.md) or
  [Register as a Student](../../student/register-as-student.md).
