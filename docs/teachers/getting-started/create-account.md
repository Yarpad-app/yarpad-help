# Create and Activate Your Teacher Account

Follow these steps to register and activate your Yarpad teacher account.

!!! important

    Students register through their teacher's registration link, not the teacher registration page.

## Step 1 — Register as a Teacher

Open the Yarpad teacher registration page. Enter your **Full name**, **Email**, and **Password**, then submit the registration form.

Your password must contain:

- At least 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character

<!-- Screenshot placeholder: Teacher registration page showing the Full name, Email, and Password fields and the password requirements. -->

## Step 2 — Sign In

After registration, Yarpad redirects you to the Login page. Sign in using the email address and password you registered with.

<!-- Screenshot placeholder: Login page with the teacher's email and password fields. -->

## Step 3 — Activate Your Account

Yarpad displays the activation code page. Enter the activation code you received by email.

<!-- Screenshot placeholder: Activation code page showing where to enter the code received by email. -->

## Step 4 — Accept Terms and Privacy Policy

review and accept its Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Read the Terms of Service.
2. Review the Privacy Policy.
3. Accept the required agreements.
4. Continue to the teacher onboarding process.

You must accept the required agreements before
proceeding with your account setup.

After accepting the agreements, continue to the teacher onboarding process.
