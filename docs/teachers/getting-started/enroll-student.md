# Enroll a Student

Teachers can create a student account from **My students**. Have the
student's full name and email address ready before you begin.

## Enroll the student

1. Sign in to Yarpad as a teacher.
2. Open the application menu and select **My students**.
3. Select **Enrollment** from the bottom navigation.
4. Enter the student's **Full name** and **Email**.
5. Select **Register** to submit the enrollment.

To close the enrollment form without submitting it, select **Cancel**.

You do not set a password in the enrollment form. Yarpad creates a student
account, generates a temporary password, and associates the student with you.
Yarpad also queues a welcome email containing the student's login
credentials. The email may take time to arrive.

## Explain the student's first sign-in

Tell the student to complete these steps after receiving the login
credentials:

1. Open the Yarpad sign-in page.
2. Sign in using the email address and temporary password from the welcome
   email.
3. Follow the **Email Verification** process when it appears.
4. Enter the activation code received by email.
5. Complete activation and sign in.

The student must activate the account before appearing in your **My students**
list. A successful enrollment submission does not make an inactive student
visible in that list.

## Manage a visible student

After the student activates the account and appears in **My students**, the
available actions are:

- **Sessions**
- **Notes**
- **Progress**
- **Unpick teacher**

## Troubleshooting

### The student is not visible in My students

Ask the student to sign in and complete the **Email Verification** process.
Newly enrolled students remain hidden from **My students** until their
accounts are activated. Yarpad does not provide a pending-student list in
this workflow.

### The welcome email has not arrived

The welcome email is queued for delivery after enrollment and may not arrive
immediately. The enrollment form does not provide a resend-welcome-email
action.

### The email address is already used

This workflow creates a new student account. An email address already used by
an account is not enrolled as a new account through this workflow. There is no
existing-student enrollment option in the enrollment form.
